# Image brief for Lovable: a new front-page picture (early-1980s receiver)

Please make **standalone image files only**: no app, page, components or code.

## Why
The current front-page picture shows a 1940s cathedral radio. The game is set in **October 1981**, so the front page should show a hi-fi of that time: the stereo receiver that would sit on a shelf behind the bar of a small-town New Hampshire inn. The old radio stays in the game's Back Room, where an antique set makes sense.

## What I need
- **2 variations** (A and B) of the one prompt below, at **1920 x 1080 (16:9)**, PNG.
- **Style reference:** attach `scene-tap-room-final.png`, the same reference as every other picture, so the grain, light and colour match.
- **Use the prompt word for word.** If you change anything, tell me exactly what in the report.

## Composition (important)
The site's title, a line of text and a button sit **on top of** this picture, in the centre. So:
- the receiver sits in the **left third** of the frame, angled slightly towards the camera;
- the **centre and right** are dark and quiet (deep wood, soft out-of-focus lamplight, a rainy window far behind), with no bright spots;
- the **VU meters and the dial glow are the brightest thing** in the picture.

## Prompt (use exactly)
> Photograph, 1981, colour negative film look, warm tungsten light, visible film grain, shallow depth of field, small-town New Hampshire. Close, low view of an early-1980s stereo receiver on a dark wooden shelf behind a small-town bar at night: a walnut wood-veneer case, a brushed-aluminium faceplate with rows of silver knobs and toggle switches, a long horizontal tuning dial glowing warm amber, and two analog VU meters lit amber with their needles mid-swing. The dial scale and the meters have no readable numbers or letters, only plain tick marks. The receiver sits in the left third of the frame; the centre and right are dark and quiet, with soft out-of-focus lamplight and a rain-streaked window far behind. Rich Kodachrome-like colour, deep browns and blacks, glowing amber. No people. No text, no logos, no watermark, nothing modern.

## Checklist before you send them
- The receiver reads clearly as **early 1980s**: walnut sides, brushed metal, VU meters. It is not a 1940s set, and not a modern one.
- **No readable text anywhere:** no brand name, no numbers on the dial or meters, no labels on the knobs.
- The centre and right third stay dark enough for white text on top.
- No people, no phones, no screens, nothing that glows blue or white.
