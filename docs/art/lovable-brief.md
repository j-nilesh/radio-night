# Image brief for Lovable: "Radio Night"

Please read all of this before generating anything. I need **standalone image files only**. Please do not build an app, a page, components or any code. I will use the images in a separate project.

We are starting with a small **pilot of two images**. Once we agree on the style, I will ask for the rest.

## 1. The project

Radio Night is a voice-first interactive murder mystery, played in a web browser. The player is a detective questioning suspects by speaking to them, and the characters answer back in voices. Think of a golden-age radio drama that listens to you. The first mystery, "The Sitting", is set in Millhaven, a tired mill town in New Hampshire, on a rainy Saturday night in October 1981. A portrait painter has been found dead in his room at the Bridgewater Inn, a two-storey clapboard inn with a bar, a snug, a kitchen and six guest rooms upstairs.

The tone is warm, dim and quietly tense, like a late-night radio play. Family-friendly (PG-13): no gore, no violence shown, no visible injury. The pictures are a quiet companion to the sound, not the main event.

## 2. How the images will be used

| Image | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| Room (16:9) | A wide banner across the top of the screen, with the room's name laid over the bottom quarter on a dark gradient |
| Portrait (4:5) | A small card, roughly 140 to 220 px wide, with the person's name and role beneath it. Also a larger dossier view |
| Evidence (1:1) | A thumbnail in a notebook, and a larger picture beside a text description |
| Hero (16:9) | The top of the home page, with the title and a button over the right-hand side |

**Important for room images: they are cropped.** The room banner is a wide, short strip: about 3:1 on a phone, up to about 5:1 on a tablet, and close to the full 16:9 only on a large screen. So the top and bottom of the picture are often cut off. Please arrange everything important in a **horizontal band across the middle of the frame (about the middle 40 percent of the height)**, slightly above the centre line, and keep the lowest quarter calm and dark. Do not place anything essential near the top or bottom edge. Images are also shown small, so they must read clearly at a glance.

## 3. The style

**A warm, grainy 1981 colour photograph.** An 80s film look: colour negative film, warm tungsten (incandescent) light from lamps, visible film grain, shallow depth of field, soft but real. It must look photographed, not painted, drawn, rendered, 3D, cartoon or "AI illustration".

- Light: warm tungsten lamps, deep shadows, a little blue from a television or a rainy window.
- Mood: dim, intimate, late night, rain.
- Colour: deep charcoal browns and blacks (`#0f0d0b`, `#1a1714`), warm amber (`#d97706`), aged cream (`#f5f0e8`). Nothing bright, neon or cold.
- Era: strictly 1981. An old CRT television, wooden paneling, patterned carpet, a rotary phone would be right. No flat screens, LEDs, smartphones, modern clothing, modern cars or logos.
- Never include any text, letters, numbers, signs, logos or watermarks inside an image.

**Style lock.** Please begin every prompt with this exact sentence, and keep it unchanged for the whole project:

> Photograph, 1981, colour negative film look, warm tungsten light, visible film grain, shallow depth of field, small-town New Hampshire.

End every prompt with: `No text, no logos, no watermark, nothing modern.`

## 7. What I will check

I will review each image against this list, so please check it yourself first.

- Nothing modern anywhere. The television is a small old CRT set.
- No garbled text, letters, numbers or logos.
- Hands and faces look natural. No extra fingers.
- The bottom quarter is calm and dark enough for a caption.
- Everything important sits in a horizontal band across the middle of the frame (about the middle 40 percent of the height), because the room banner crops the top and bottom.
- Grain and warm tungsten light feel like film, not a filter.
- Ray looks decent and tired, not shifty or menacing.
