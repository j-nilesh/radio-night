# Lovable's terms, as checked
Checked on 26 November 2026 at https://lovable.dev/terms. This is a record kept for the project, not legal advice.

## Who owns the pictures
Under "Ownership", "Your Rights", the Terms say:

> As between us, you also own any AI Output generated for you through the Services, subject to any third-party rights in the underlying models, training data, or outputs.

The final pictures were generated for the project owner through Lovable's Platform, so the owner owns them as between the owner and Lovable.

## What the Terms do not promise
Under "AI Output", the Terms say output may be similar or identical to content generated for other users who submit similar prompts, and that Lovable does not guarantee it is unique or free of third-party rights. The owner reviewed every picture before it shipped.

## What was also said, and not kept
On 13 November 2026 Lovable's built-in assistant, asked whether the generated images could be used commercially and published in open-source repositories, answered that the owner owns them and is free to do both. A screenshot of that chat was first kept here and then removed, because it also showed other, unrelated projects in the sidebar. The Terms above are the source relied on.

They are included in the repository only so the game runs. All rights in them are reserved by the owner; see NOTICE.
